# WANAIA — Product Vision

## Discover. Understand. Compare. Buy. Own. Move.

---

## 1. What is WANAIA?

WANAIA is the **intelligence layer for mobility**. It is not a car marketplace. It is a platform that transforms how people discover, understand, compare, buy, own, and maintain vehicles — across every category of personal and commercial mobility.

WANAIA combines structured vehicle intelligence, marketplace capabilities, ownership tools, and AI-powered advisory into a single, trusted platform designed for global scale.

---

## 2. Mission

> **Empower every person to make confident mobility decisions — whether buying their first car, switching to electric, choosing a motorcycle, or managing a fleet.**

---

## 3. Core User Journey

```
DISCOVER → UNDERSTAND → COMPARE → DECIDE → BUY → VERIFY → OWN → MAINTAIN → SELL → REPLACE
```

WANAIA creates value at **every stage** of this lifecycle, not just at the point of purchase.

## 5. Core Product Pillars

### 5.1 Vehicle Intelligence
- Structured vehicle knowledge graph (Brand → Model → Generation → Trim → Specs)
- Market-aware data (pricing, availability, features vary by country)
- Reliability intelligence from owner data
- Safety ratings and analysis

### 5.2 Smart Discovery & Search
- Natural language search ("reliable family SUV under 300,000 MAD")
- Faceted filtering with intelligent defaults
- Semantic search architecture
- Personalized recommendations

### 5.3 WANAIA AI Advisor
- Conversational mobility advisor
- Understands user context (family, budget, usage, location)
- Recommends based on structured data — never invents facts
- Explains trade-offs transparently

### 5.4 Marketplace
- Used vehicle listings with trust indicators
- Dealer profiles and inventory
- Buyer request marketplace (inverted model)
- WANAIA Best Offer (competitive dealer offers)
- Deal Score (market-based price analysis)

### 5.5 Comparison Engine
- Side-by-side comparison (2–4+ vehicles)
- Personalized weighted comparison
- TCO comparison
- Reliability comparison

### 5.6 Ownership Platform
- My Garage (personal vehicle dashboard)
- Maintenance tracking and reminders
- Vehicle Passport (digital identity)
- Insurance and document management
- Expense tracking

### 5.7 Content & Community
- Editorial content connected to vehicle graph
- Owner reviews and experiences
- Vehicle-specific communities
- Buying guides and maintenance guides

## 9. Design Principles

1. **Intelligence over information** — Don't just show data; provide insight.
2. **Trust is non-negotiable** — Never fabricate, exaggerate, or mislead.
3. **Global from day one** — Architecture supports any language, market, currency.
4. **Full lifecycle** — Value before, during, and after purchase.
5. **AI as advisor, not salesperson** — AI recommends honestly, explains trade-offs.
6. **Privacy by design** — Minimize data collection, maximize user control.
7. **Mobile-first, not mobile-only** — Premium experience on every device.
8. **Data quality over quantity** — Clearly label confidence levels and sources.
