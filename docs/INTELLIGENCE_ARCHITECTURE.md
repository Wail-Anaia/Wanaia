# WANAIA — Intelligence Architecture

## The Mobility Intelligence Pipeline

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Principle

WANAIA is not a superficial car marketplace or a generic LLM wrapper. WANAIA is the **intelligence layer for mobility**.

### The Fundamental Architectural Principle
> **The AI (LLM / Agent) is NOT the intelligence engine.**  
> The AI is an **interactive presentation, reasoning, and conversational interface** that consumes deterministic, verified, structured, and auditable intelligence generated by the **WANAIA Intelligence Pipeline**.

The intelligence pipeline transforms disparate, unstructured, and multi-source raw automotive and mobility data into verified facts, statistical observations, deterministic calculations, composite intelligence scores, and actionable decision criteria.

## 2. Layer-by-Layer Architecture & Responsibilities

### Layer 1: Raw Data Sources
Connects to external reality across disparate data formats:
- **Manufacturer Official Data:** Catalogs, homologation sheets, WLTP/EPA test figures, warranty books.
- **Marketplace & Dealer Feeds:** Dealer inventory management systems (DMS), CSV/XML feeds, private seller submissions.
- **Regulatory & Government Databases:** Homologation registries, crash safety test results (Euro NCAP, NHTSA), road tax tables, regional EV incentive schedules.
- **Owner & Community Telemetry/Surveys:** Real-world fuel/energy consumption logs, maintenance records, verified ownership reviews.
- **Market Transaction Data:** Historic auction records, listed asking prices, residual value tables.

### Layer 2: Data Ingestion Layer
- **Decoupled Connectors (Adapters):** Distinct extractors for each provider/source format.
- **Ingestion Staging Buffer:** Raw payloads are stored immutably with SHA-256 signatures before mutation.
- **Rate-Limiting & Backoff:** Resilient scheduled and event-driven pull/push pipelines.

### Layer 3: Data Quality & Verification Layer
- **Syntactic Validation:** Schema conformance (e.g. valid VIN regex, non-negative horsepower, plausible dimensions).
- **Semantic Validation & Plausibility:** Detects anomalies (e.g. a B-segment hatchback claiming 900 hp or 1.2 L/100km fuel consumption without plug-in hybrid powertrain).
- **Conflict Detection:** Identifies discrepancies between manufacturer claims and regulatory homologation or multiple dealer submissions.
- **Confidence Rating Assignment:** Assigns an epistemic confidence score (`VERIFIED`, `HIGH`, `ESTIMATED`, `DISPUTED`).

### Layer 4: Normalization & Canonicalization Layer
- **Taxonomy Resolution:** Maps localized naming conventions to the canonical WANAIA ontology (e.g. "Clio V Phase 2 TCe 90 Equilibre" maps to Brand:`Renault`, Model:`Clio`, Generation:`Clio V Facelift`, Trim:`Equilibre`, Powertrain:`1.0 TCe 90 Petrol Manual`).
- **Standardization:** Normalizes units (kW ↔ hp, L/100km ↔ MPG ↔ km/L, mm ↔ inches, currency conversions with date indexing).
- **Market Localization:** Maps canonical variants to market-specific availability and local tax brackets (e.g. Moroccan fiscal horsepower / "Puissance Fiscale" `CV`).

### Layer 5: Data Provenance & Trust Layer
*(Detailed in `TRUST_AND_DATA_PROVENANCE.md`)*
- Attaches metadata to every attribute: `source_id`, `source_type`, `collected_at`, `valid_from`, `valid_to`, `verification_status`, and `epistemic_type` (`FACT`, `OBSERVATION`, `CALCULATION`, `INTELLIGENCE`, `OPINION`).

### Layer 6: Mobility Knowledge Model
- The relational and graph-structured representation of all mobility entities (Brands, Models, Generations, Variants/Trims, Powertrains, Specifications, Features, Markets, Historical Timelines).

### Layer 7: Intelligence Engine (Domain Analytics)
Processes normalized facts into domain insights:
- **Market Price Distribution Engine:** Computes real-time market medians, percentiles, depreciation curves, and inventory velocity for given segments and trims.
- **Reliability Aggregator:** Combines owner reports, inspection statistics, and known technical service bulletins (TSBs) into component-level failure probability indexes.
- **TCO Matrix Calculator:** Computes fuel/energy expenditure, preventative maintenance schedules, tire replacement intervals, insurance estimates, and tax costs over 1, 3, and 5-year horizons.

### Layer 8: Decision Engine (Deterministic & Versioned)
*(Detailed in `DECISION_ENGINE.md`)*
- Pure, deterministic, version-controlled mathematical and rules-based algorithms that calculate composite scores (`WANAIA Score`, `Deal Score`, `Reliability Score`, `Personal Fit Score`, `EV Fit Score`, `Rider Fit Score`).
- Does **not** use generative AI; produces exact, reproducible numeric scores with full input attribution.

### Layer 9: Explanation Engine
- Takes the outputs of the Decision Engine and generates structured, deterministic **Why** (Strengths) and **Why Not** (Drawbacks/Trade-offs) statements.
- Prepares structured justification matrices for human consumption and AI grounding.

### Layer 10: Personalization Layer
- Evaluates the user's explicit **Mobility Profile** (`MOBILITY_PROFILE.md`) against the vehicle's attributes and intelligence metrics to compute individualized scores and personalized warnings.

### Layer 11: WANAIA AI Advisor & Presentation Layer
- Consumes the structured outputs of Layers 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10.
- Converts structured intelligence into fluent, multi-lingual (Arabic, French, English), empathetic, and interactive explanations without ever creating facts from thin air.

## 3. Strict Boundary Rules

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                             CRITICAL BOUNDARIES                          │
├─────────────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────┤
│ CAN DO / MUST DO                        │ STRICTLY FORBIDDEN             │
├─────────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────┤
│ Intelligence Engine calculates TCO and  │ AI guessing or estimating      │
│ depreciation from historical data       │ running costs or fuel economy  │
├─────────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────┤
│ Decision Engine calculates WANAIA Score │ LLM assigning arbitrary 1-10   │
│ with versioned mathematical formulas    │ scores based on its prompt     │
├─────────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────┤
│ Explanation Engine outputs structured   │ AI inventing trade-offs or     │
│ pros and cons based on benchmark bounds │ features not in the database   │
├─────────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────┤
│ AI translates structured intelligence   │ AI performing direct SQL or    │
│ into interactive natural language       │ modifying vehicle facts        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────┘
```
